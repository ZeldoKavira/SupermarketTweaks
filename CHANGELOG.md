# Changelog

The release workflow publishes the top section of this file as the Nexus changelog, so keep the
newest version first and write it for players rather than for the repo.

## 1.0.1

No gameplay changes. First release published through the automated pipeline, to prove it works
end to end.

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Auto-pricing at a set percentage of market value, reapplied each day and on new products.
- Restock All button and F8, ordering against shelf capacity plus a configurable minimum.
- F9 and a machine-panel button to queue production runs, spread across all machines.
- Manufactured shelf tags, stock readouts and a research tracker.
- Automatic staff role switching for cashiers, security, storage, technicians, order fillers and
  manufacturing staff.
- F5 and F6 speed tiers that survive the end-of-day reset, with a drop to 1x while the anti-theft
  alarm is going.
- Sales restored each morning, or filled with your most expensive products.
- Placement overlap tolerance for grid-snapped shelves.
